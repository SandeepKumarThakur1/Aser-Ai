Jags Rao

Founder & CEO
FOUNDER & LEADERSHIP

Jags Rao is a serial entrepreneur and the visionary behind Aser AI Sdn. Bhd., founded in Kuala Lumpur in 2024 to transform risk management through cutting-edge AI and data-driven intelligence. With a global career spanning technology, insurance innovation, and strategic growth, Jags is known for orchestrating high-impact ecosystems and driving cross-industry value chains powered by data and digital platforms. Raised in India, shaped by experiences in Japan and the U.S., and based in Switzerland since 2001, he brings a truly global perspective to building the future of tech-enabled businesses.

Key Experience

  • Ex-Swiss Re – Head of New Propositions
  • Co-founded reinsurance industry consortium on blockchain
  • 2X Entrepreneur with tech start-up experience
  • Technology sales to Fortune 500 customers

Expertise

Insurance Business · Energy Transition · ESG · Agriculture · Technology Innovation
