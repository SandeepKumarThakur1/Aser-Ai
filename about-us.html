WHY WE EXIST

Better visibility is only
the beginning.

Organisations collect more data than ever. Yet early signs of risk often remain disconnected from the decisions that could change the outcome.

We founded ASER AI to help close that gap—connecting what assets reveal today with what organisations need to anticipate tomorrow.

Our purpose: turn earlier understanding into better-informed action.

WHO WE ARE

Different disciplines.
One shared direction.

ASER AI is a Kuala Lumpur-headquartered global company with offices in Singapore and Switzerland, bringing together experience across insurance, technology, asset engineering and business leadership.

We are building Pantheon, our Predictive Risk Intelligence platform, to connect asset behaviour with operational and financial consequences—helping owners, operators and insurance partners understand where risk is heading.

WHERE WE FOCUS

Starting with batteries.
Building towards broader applications.

Battery energy storage is our initial focus: critical infrastructure where safety, performance and financial exposure are closely connected.

Pantheon complements existing BMS, EMS and SCADA systems, helping teams anticipate developing issues and protect performance throughout an asset's remaining useful life.

Our research explores how the same disciplines could address emerging risks across mobility, the built environment and financial systems.
